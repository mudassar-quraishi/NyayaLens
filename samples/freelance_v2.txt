FREELANCE SERVICE AGREEMENT — VERSION 2 (REVISED)

This Freelance Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1st June 2025 ("Effective Date") by and between:

CLIENT: Pinnacle Marketing Solutions LLP, having its registered office at 23, MG Road, Pune, Maharashtra – 411001 (hereinafter "the Client" or "Party A")

AND

FREELANCER: Ms. Ananya Iyer, residing at 56, Anna Nagar, Chennai, Tamil Nadu – 600040 (hereinafter "the Freelancer" or "Party B")

WHEREAS the Client wishes to engage the Freelancer to provide certain services, and the Freelancer agrees to provide such services, the parties agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Freelancer shall provide social media management, content creation, and digital marketing services as described in the Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A. The Client may modify the scope of services at any time by providing written notice to the Freelancer, and the Freelancer shall comply with such modifications without additional compensation unless the modification substantially increases the volume of work, in which case the parties shall negotiate in good faith.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall be effective from 1st June 2025 and shall continue for a period of 12 (twelve) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein. The Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 6-month periods unless the Freelancer provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry.

3. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the Freelancer a monthly retainer of Rs. 75,000/- (Rupees Seventy-Five Thousand Only). Payment shall be made within 60 (sixty) days of receipt of a valid invoice from the Freelancer. Invoices shall be submitted on the 1st of each month for the preceding month's services. The Client may withhold payment if it reasonably believes that the services do not meet the quality standards, and shall notify the Freelancer of the deficiency within the payment period.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All deliverables created by the Freelancer under this Agreement, including drafts, concepts, and work-in-progress, shall become the exclusive property of the Client upon creation, regardless of payment status. The Freelancer shall not use the deliverables in their portfolio without the Client's prior written consent. The Freelancer warrants that all deliverables are original and do not infringe any third-party rights.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties shall maintain the confidentiality of proprietary information exchanged during the term of this Agreement and for a period of 2 (two) years thereafter. Neither party shall disclose such information to third parties without prior written consent.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
The Freelancer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client, its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorney fees) arising out of or relating to the Freelancer's services, deliverables, or breach of this Agreement, without any limitation on the amount. The Client's liability to the Freelancer shall not exceed the fees paid in the month preceding the claim.

7. TERMINATION
The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing 15 (fifteen) days' written notice to the Freelancer, without cause. The Freelancer may terminate this Agreement only for cause, by providing 60 (sixty) days' written notice and only if the Client has failed to cure the breach within 30 days of receiving notice. Upon termination by the Client, the Client shall pay for services rendered up to the date of termination, minus any deductions for deficient work. Upon termination by the Freelancer, the Freelancer shall complete all ongoing projects and shall not be entitled to payment for the notice period if the Client waives the notice.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved through arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The arbitration shall be conducted in Mumbai, Maharashtra, with a sole arbitrator appointed by the Client.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. The courts at Mumbai, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes.

10. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations due to circumstances beyond their reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, war, pandemic, or government actions. The affected party shall notify the other party within 7 days.

11. NON-COMPETE
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of 12 months thereafter, the Freelancer shall not provide similar services to any direct competitor of the Client, as determined by the Client.

12. GENERAL PROVISIONS
(a) This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
(b) The Client may amend the terms of this Agreement by providing 15 days' written notice to the Freelancer. Continued provision of services after such notice constitutes acceptance of the amended terms.
(c) The Client may assign this Agreement to any affiliate or successor. The Freelancer may not assign this Agreement without the Client's written consent.
(d) Notices shall be sent to the addresses stated above or to such other address as may be notified in writing.
