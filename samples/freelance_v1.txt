FREELANCE SERVICE AGREEMENT — VERSION 1

This Freelance Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1st June 2025 ("Effective Date") by and between:

CLIENT: Pinnacle Marketing Solutions LLP, having its registered office at 23, MG Road, Pune, Maharashtra – 411001 (hereinafter "the Client" or "Party A")

AND

FREELANCER: Ms. Ananya Iyer, residing at 56, Anna Nagar, Chennai, Tamil Nadu – 600040 (hereinafter "the Freelancer" or "Party B")

WHEREAS the Client wishes to engage the Freelancer to provide certain services, and the Freelancer agrees to provide such services, the parties agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Freelancer shall provide social media management, content creation, and digital marketing services as described in the Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A. Any changes to the scope of services shall be documented in a written amendment signed by both parties.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall be effective from 1st June 2025 and shall continue for a period of 12 (twelve) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein. The Agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent.

3. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the Freelancer a monthly retainer of Rs. 75,000/- (Rupees Seventy-Five Thousand Only). Payment shall be made within 15 (fifteen) days of receipt of a valid invoice from the Freelancer. Invoices shall be submitted on the 1st of each month for the preceding month's services. Late payments shall attract an interest of 1.5% per month.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All deliverables created by the Freelancer under this Agreement shall become the property of the Client upon full payment. The Freelancer retains the right to use the deliverables in their portfolio. The Freelancer warrants that all deliverables are original and do not infringe any third-party rights.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties shall maintain the confidentiality of proprietary information exchanged during the term of this Agreement and for a period of 2 (two) years thereafter. Neither party shall disclose such information to third parties without prior written consent.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
The Freelancer's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid to the Freelancer in the 3 (three) months preceding the claim. Each party shall indemnify the other against claims arising from their negligence or breach of this Agreement.

7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 (thirty) days' written notice to the other party. Upon termination, the Client shall pay for all services rendered up to the date of termination. The Freelancer shall return all Client materials within 15 days of termination.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through mediation. If mediation fails within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The arbitration shall be conducted in Pune, Maharashtra.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. The courts at Pune, Maharashtra shall have jurisdiction over any disputes not resolved through arbitration.

10. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations due to circumstances beyond their reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, war, pandemic, or government actions. The affected party shall notify the other party within 7 days.

11. GENERAL PROVISIONS
(a) This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
(b) Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
(c) Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent.
(d) Notices shall be sent to the addresses stated above or to such other address as may be notified in writing.
