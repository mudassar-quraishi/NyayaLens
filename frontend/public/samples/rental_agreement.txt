RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on this 1st day of January, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

LANDLORD: Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, residing at 45, Vasant Kunj, New Delhi – 110070 (hereinafter referred to as "the Landlord" or "Party A")

AND

TENANT: Ms. Priya Mehta, residing at 12, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi – 110024 (hereinafter referred to as "the Tenant" or "Party B")

The parties agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. PROPERTY DESCRIPTION
The Landlord hereby lets out and the Tenant hereby takes on rent the residential premises situated at Flat No. 302, Tower B, Green Valley Apartments, Sector 56, Gurugram, Haryana – 122011, measuring approximately 1200 sq. ft., including two bedrooms, one drawing room, kitchen, two bathrooms, and one balcony (hereinafter referred to as "the Premises").

2. TERM OF AGREEMENT
This Agreement shall be valid for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st January 2025 and ending on 30th November 2025. Upon expiry, the Agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent of both parties, subject to revised terms as agreed upon.

3. RENT AND PAYMENT
The monthly rent for the Premises shall be Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand Only), payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. The rent shall be paid via bank transfer to the Landlord's designated account. A late payment fee of Rs. 500/- per day shall be charged for any payment received after the 7th of the month. The Landlord reserves the right to increase the rent by up to 20% upon renewal of this Agreement, at the Landlord's sole discretion, without requiring the Tenant's consent.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,50,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only), equivalent to six months' rent, at the time of signing this Agreement. The security deposit shall be refundable at the end of the tenancy period, subject to deductions for any damages, unpaid rent, unpaid bills, or any other outstanding dues as determined by the Landlord. The Landlord shall determine the condition of the Premises and the amount of deductions at his sole discretion.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall be responsible for all minor repairs and maintenance of the Premises, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical fittings, painting, and fixtures. Any repair costing above Rs. 5,000/- shall require prior written approval from the Landlord. Major structural repairs shall be the responsibility of the Landlord, provided the damage is not caused by the Tenant's negligence. The Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good and habitable condition throughout the tenancy.

6. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and her immediate family members. The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, or transfer the Premises or any part thereof to any third party without the prior written consent of the Landlord. The Tenant shall not use the Premises for any illegal, immoral, or commercial activity. The Tenant shall comply with all rules and regulations of the housing society.

7. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND EARLY TERMINATION
There shall be a lock-in period of 6 (six) months from the commencement date of this Agreement, during which neither party may terminate this Agreement. If the Tenant wishes to vacate the Premises before the expiry of the lock-in period, the Tenant shall forfeit the entire security deposit as liquidated damages and shall additionally pay a penalty equal to 3 (three) months' rent. After the lock-in period, the Tenant must provide 90 days' written notice to vacate.

8. LANDLORD'S RIGHT OF ACCESS
The Landlord or his authorized representative shall have the right to enter and inspect the Premises at any time, with or without prior notice to the Tenant, for the purpose of inspection, repairs, or showing the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers. The Tenant shall cooperate and provide access as required by the Landlord.

9. UTILITIES AND BILLS
The Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utility bills including electricity, water, gas, internet, and DTH/cable charges during the period of tenancy. All utility connections shall remain in the name of the Landlord. The Tenant shall ensure timely payment of all bills and shall provide proof of payment upon request.

10. TERMINATION BY LANDLORD
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing 30 days' written notice to the Tenant. Upon such termination, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises within the notice period. The Landlord may also terminate this Agreement immediately without notice in the event of breach of any terms by the Tenant, including but not limited to non-payment of rent for more than 15 days, subletting without permission, or causing damage to the Premises.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved through mutual discussion between the parties. If the dispute cannot be resolved amicably within 30 days, either party may seek legal remedy in the courts of New Delhi. The Tenant agrees to bear all legal costs and attorney fees incurred by the Landlord in enforcing this Agreement.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, including the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 and the applicable rent control legislation of the state of Haryana. The courts at New Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes arising from this Agreement.

13. GENERAL PROVISIONS
(a) This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
(b) Any modification to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.
(c) If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
(d) The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations or additions to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any alterations made by the Tenant shall become the property of the Landlord upon termination of this Agreement.
